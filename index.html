Quantum Materials & Spintronics

Research Areas & Frontiers

Engineering novel quantum behavior at atomically sharp 3d–4d/5d oxide interfaces.

"More is different."
— Philip W. Anderson (Nobel Laureate in Physics)

Interfacial Ferromagnetism

Investigating emergent ferromagnetic ordering at nominally non-ferromagnetic oxide interfaces. By controlling exchange interactions, orbital reconstruction, and lattice strain, we stabilize ferromagnetic ordering confined to a few unit cells at the interface.

Spin–Orbit Coupling & Rashba Effect

Exploring how inversion symmetry breaking and strong relativistic spin-orbit coupling at 4d/5d oxide interfaces induce momentum-dependent spin textures, topological Hall effect, and spin-charge interconversion phenomena.

Spin Hall Magnetoresistance (SMR)

Probing spin current transmission, reflection, and absorption across oxide heterostructures. Utilizing atomistic interface engineering to tune spin transparency and realize sign switching of SMR for oxide spintronics.
